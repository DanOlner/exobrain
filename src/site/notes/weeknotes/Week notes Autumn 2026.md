I'm going to try it for four weeks and see if it works for my gadfly brain. I like the idea, but let's see how the do actually does.

Feel like I should add a fourth heading, something like "empty howl into the void" or "thoughts while sitting in pants eating pringles". A heading to provide space for absolute, mind-numbing vacuity of no instrumental worth whatsoever. Let's see.

That may end up feeling a bit too much like being at school, but I like the idea of having some structure to it. Let's see. Err.
